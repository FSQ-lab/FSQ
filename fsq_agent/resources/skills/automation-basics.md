# Automation Basics

- Use the platform tools exposed by the active harness for automation interactions. Do not invent actions outside the provided platform tools.
- Prefer locators from current platform tool responses, observations, and action evidence. These have higher priority than knowledge because knowledge can be stale; use knowledge only as flow reference and candidate target guidance.
- Use platform tool assertion or verification capabilities for verify, assert, confirm, check, ensure, and validate requirements. Do not decide pass or fail only from agent narrative, screenshots, stale artifacts, or ad-hoc observation.
- Use deterministic assertions for exact accessible text, numeric values, checked/selected states, and other exposed semantic facts. Complex or ambiguous locators require renewed observation and refinement, not visual approximation. Missing or clipped evidence is not proof that no accessibility node exists.
- Reserve `assert_with_ai` for explicitly visual assertions of image content, colors/appearance, or custom-drawn content without accessible semantic state. Do not use it to locate targets, ground coordinates, drive the UI, replace an available deterministic check, or override deterministic counterevidence. The presence of unrelated accessible nodes does not invalidate a genuinely visual assertion.
- On Android, the separate read-only `inspect_with_ai` observation can extract genuinely pixel-only content with `mode: "extract"`. Use `mode: "locate"` only for inaccessible targets or targets unresolved after fresh accessibility observation and locator refinement. It is not an assertion and never clicks or swipes automatically. Only its current SDK-returned `pointer_center` may feed an explicit `tap_at` or explicit-point `swipe`; do not infer pointer coordinates from image bounds or reuse stale candidates. Other platforms' tool and assertion rules are unchanged.
- Preserve an unsaved, unsubmitted, or unsent form only when the goal explicitly requests that restriction or a draft state (for example "do not save", "without submitting", "leave as draft", or "unsent"). Those explicit restrictions take precedence; do not infer them from the mere absence of "click Submit".
- Otherwise, when a requested entry, setting, or interactive form/challenge answer needs a clear Submit / Save / Done / OK control to become effective, complete that task-required action and verify its resulting state. A populated field alone is not that completed result. Do not extend this rule to unrelated or destructive confirmations.
- In click-to-reveal sequences, distinguish requested click count from observed value count: the initial visible item may be part of the valid sequence, and the final click may open the answer form instead of revealing another value. Use the complete actually observed valid sequence and complete required submission/result verification; do not invent an extra item or abandon submission solely because the form appeared. Genuine missing/conflicting observations remain unverified.
- Honor supplied `write_budget` max/remaining facts; they are limits, not permission to reset, refund, or increase quota. Reuse read-only observation, inspection, and assertions to resolve uncertainty before scarce writes. Reserve only goal-required commits; never reserve or perform an explicitly prohibited save/submit.
- Keep a concise plan/context ledger across screens: observed identities, relevant fields, unresolved facts, and coverage. Retain distinct occurrences and multiplicity, but reconcile overlap so paging does not count the same occurrence twice. Reanchor targets from current observations after paging or deletion; old viewport positions are stale.
- A visible difference permits pruning only when it logically disproves the goal-defined equality/qualification predicate. Clipping is not a mismatch; unequal durations can still contribute to sum/subset tasks. Do not stop inconclusive with feasible remaining requested coverage and adequate supplied budget.
- Preserve an explicitly requested complete filename exactly, including an extensionless name. A default `.md` suffix is not equivalent. Adjust format or use an authorized rename when needed; after authorized saving/renaming, verify full name and destination in the actual file-list/file-management view, not an editor title that may hide extensions. Draft/no-save restrictions prohibit extra saves or renames to satisfy this guidance.
- For location search, begin with the core requested name while preserving country/region constraints. Expand the current general search radius through observed ranges until a candidate, observed exhausted/unchanged/blocked range, or supplied limit before trying narrow category tabs. Never repeat unchanged search cycles, invent hidden coordinates, or assume unobserved geography.
- Do not rewrite successfully verified input because a narrow field visually clips it. Use allowed same-target readback to resolve display uncertainty. If acceptance is uncertain rather than verified, establish what happened before considering any replay; do not treat possible acceptance as a known rejection.
